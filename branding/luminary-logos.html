Brand Identity Exploration

LUMINARY

A learning schedule orchestrator where every learner becomes a guiding light. Below are six logo concepts exploring different facets of the brand.

A four-pointed star ascending through orbital rings — representing a learner rising above the noise. The orbits symbolize structured learning paths, while the star is the individual emerging into brilliance. Clean geometric shape ensures scalability down to 14px favicons.

A bold geometric "L" monogram where miniature stars replace traditional serifs. The letterform feels architectural and grounded — structure — while the stars add lightness and aspiration. The L works beautifully as a standalone app icon.

Concept 03

The Beacon

"Your guiding light in learning"
Primary
Nebula Theme
Aurora Theme

A beacon of light — a flame that doubles as a star — radiating outward. This concept leans into the "guiding light" meaning of Luminary. The shape evokes both a lighthouse beacon and a candle flame. Adapts beautifully to each theme color.

Concept 04

The Constellation Path

"Every star connected, every step intentional"
Primary
Compact Mark
With Wordmark
Luminary

An ascending constellation — nodes of increasing brightness connected by lines, culminating in a brilliant star. This directly visualizes the learning journey: small beginnings, connected steps, growing mastery. Each node is a skill; the final star is the Luminary you become.

Concept 05

The Solar Arch

"The arc of mastery"
Primary
Minimal
Sunrise Theme
Brand Color Palette

A parabolic arch with a star at its apex — like a star clearing the horizon at dawn. The arc represents the learning curve (deliberately not a straight line — mastery is curved, organic). Dots along the path represent milestones. The most minimal and elegant of the concepts.

Concept 06

The Wordmark

"The name IS the logo"
Primary — Dark
Light Background
Stacked / Mobile
Luminary
Become the light

A pure typographic wordmark where the "y" in Luminary transforms into gold, crowned with a four-pointed star. The name does the heavy lifting — no symbol needed. The star-on-y becomes a recognizable micro-brand element. "Luminar" in white anchors trust; the golden "y" adds aspiration. Perfect for header bars, splash screens, and app stores.

Recommended Direction

Concept 01 (Rising Star) as the primary icon mark
Concept 06 (Wordmark) as the primary logotype

Used together they create a flexible brand system:
Icon mark for favicons, app icons, and avatars
Wordmark for headers, splash screens, and documentation
Stacked version for mobile and social media

Brand palette: Gold (#f5b942) as primary, Blue (#4f7df7) as accent, Near-black (#0a0a0f) and Off-white (#f8f8fc) as backgrounds.
